# Approved-reference translation

Approved downstream anchors and contracts must not move to fit a failed derivative unless the user separately authorizes an intent change. For each materially different output kind or target form, produce one representative and verify it in its native target form and the minimum actual-use context needed for the next claim before dependent fan-out.

Open the approved source alone before the candidate and freeze 3-7 identity-carrying observables. Include applicable silhouette and negative space, part order and connectivity, relative spans, thicknesses, or visible masses, and orientation-specific landmarks. Then inspect the target at native and nearest-neighbor enlarged scale; for each observable record what the target preserves, merges, reorders, or inverts.

When semantic identity or unaided readability is required for a small or symbolic target, also obtain a candidate-only, uninformed reading at native scale before that reviewer sees any labeled or side-by-side comparison. Record the strongest object or identity reading, confidence, and applicable confusable alternatives. The completion agent still performs the source-first structural inventory; use a separate fresh reviewer when it already knows the intended identity or has seen the source. Identity that depends on labels, source adjacency, or enlargement is not a PASS; absent reliable uninformed evidence, that property is UNVERIFIED. Give each output kind and each required direction an independent verdict. One output's PASS does not upgrade another output or direction.

For a multi-variant or multi-state family, use the sourced invariant, variant-dependent, and state-dependent matrix from the user or design contract. Check shared invariants across outputs, but check variant and state properties against their named contract—not the source example's literal label, content, style, difficulty, or action. Do not invent permitted variation from a candidate. A missing variation or state contract is UNVERIFIED.

Generic resemblance or the presence of named or color-coded parts is not fidelity. A required identity relation that materially collapses or inverts is FAIL. Explicitly nonessential detail may be omitted; do not invent pixel-perfect fidelity beyond the approved contract and target form.

If representative verification remains unresolved, whether objective or subjective, pause dependent fan-out. Ask the user only when the remaining judgment is subjective intent; resolve objective evidence without another approval gate. A numeric proxy does not override the approved reference; outside the exact verified target and state it remains a hypothesis.

The request authorizes the bounded translation, not unrelated intent changes. Do not add repeated approval gates. When a follow-up variant or task must reuse the approved reference, identify it by a stable project-owned path and version; a temporary attachment path is not durable evidence.
