# Direction and delivery scope

Link the existing Product authority; this is not a second product specification.
For each approved milestone or release, distinguish required outcomes from
optional/later ideas. Give each required outcome a check and actual evidence
or an explicit unverified state. The milestone/release is complete only after
all required outcomes meet their checks, or an authorized decision changes the
scope with its reason recorded. Do not silently drop an unfinished item.
Record explicit direction decisions, scope changes and remaining priorities.
Updating evidence for an already approved required item does not itself change
direction or scope; use actual verification, not task narration alone.
Individual task completion belongs in the task source and does not by itself
complete a milestone. Routine bug fixes and discussion alone do not change
direction.
